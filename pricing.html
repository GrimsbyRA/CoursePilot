What to know before buying

Are the resources ready to deliver without checking?

No. They are editable starter materials, not guaranteed complete or compliant courses. You must verify facts, source dates, regulations, assessment answers, accessibility and suitability before teaching.

Does a CoursePilot certificate prove accreditation?

No. A template does not confer a qualification or accreditation. Only state accreditation or awarding-body approval where you are authorised and can substantiate the claim.

How will I receive a paid pack?

The launch version is planned as a manually fulfilled service: Lopay collects the payment; the operator must confirm the order and supply the agreed pack using the customer contact details collected through the agreed process. The operator must add a working contact address and a clear delivery timeframe before checkout is enabled.

Can I cancel the membership?

The monthly plan must have a clear cancellation route and terms before it is enabled. Do not subscribe until the final checkout and cancellation instructions are visible.

What about refunds?

The final checkout and terms must explain the refund and cancellation policy, including applicable UK consumer rights. Nothing on this page removes statutory rights.
