Built around current information — not yesterday’s materials

Training materials should reflect the latest relevant legislation, regulations, guidance, statistics, facts and figures. CoursePilot’s planned research and review workflow is designed to help providers identify what needs checking before a course goes live.

⚖️

Legislation and regulations

Identify the relevant laws, regulations, official guidance and review dates for your course topic, with source links where available.

📊

Current facts and figures

Build in relevant statistics and factual context, with the source and publication date recorded so a trainer can verify them.

🔎

Sources and review checks

Make it easier to spot outdated references and flag technical or safety-critical information for competent review before delivery.

Important: the current prototype does not automatically research or verify live legislation, regulations or statistics. Always check authoritative, current sources and have a competent person review course materials before use.

SAMPLE RESOURCE · DRAFT

Manual Handling Awareness

Draft session plan · 90 minutes · Introductory level

Learning objectives

  • Identify common manual handling risk factors.
  • Explain why the task, load, environment and individual should be considered.
  • Describe when to stop and seek help or use handling aids.

Proposed session structure

TimeActivityMethod
0–10 minWelcome and prior knowledgeDiscussion
10–30 minRisk factors and planningTrainer input
30–55 minScenario-based hazard spottingGroup activity
55–75 minControls and safe systemsDiscussion/demo
75–90 minKnowledge check and recapQuiz

Sample knowledge-check questions

  1. Name two factors to consider before moving a load.
  2. What should you do if a load is unstable or too heavy to manage safely?
  3. Why should handling aids and workplace procedures be considered?

Trainer review: Check this draft against current guidance, site-specific procedures and learner needs before delivery.

Frequently asked questions


Does CoursePilot generate AI content automatically?
Not yet. This prototype creates a course request summary in your browser. You can copy it into an AI tool manually. An AI API integration would need to be built and tested separately.


Can I use the outputs as accredited training?
No accreditation is provided. Draft resources must be checked by a competent trainer against applicable standards, current guidance and the needs of the course.


Can I subscribe or pay now?
No. The prices are proposed examples. Payment processing and account management are not connected.


Is my form submitted to CoursePilot?
No. The form runs in your browser and creates a local summary only. It does not transmit or persist the information.
