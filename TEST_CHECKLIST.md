# CoursePilot test checklist — question bank

1. Publish the updated files and open Studio.
2. Choose First Aid, select 100 questions, generate the pack. Confirm the quiz reports only the number of unique questions available and displays a bank-limit warning rather than repeating questions.
3. Repeat for Manual Handling, Working at Height, COSHH, Food Hygiene/Allergens and Forklift.
4. Check question numbering, all three options, answer letter and explanation for every item.
5. Confirm the correct answer letter matches the intended option.
6. Download the full pack and individual quiz output; open both and confirm text is complete and readable.
7. Test other subjects and ensure the output clearly shows its smaller generic starter bank if the requested size cannot be met.
8. Have a competent subject-matter expert verify every question and answer against current authoritative sources and the relevant syllabus before formal use.

Acceptance rule: never repeat or paraphrase items merely to reach a selected count. If the available bank is smaller than the request, disclose the limit.
